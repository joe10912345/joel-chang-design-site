T. Interceptor MP

Autonomous AI-powered drone, guarding airspace with speed and agility.

As drone threats grow more prevalent, T.Interceptor MP is a multi-functional defense system built for urban security and critical infrastructure protection. It integrates AI image recognition, modular design, and cloud communication to perform real-time detection, monitoring, and interception in complex environments.

Powered by AI flight control and visual recognition, the system autonomously detects and locks onto suspicious aerial targets without continuous human oversight. Once confirmed, it launches a physical capture net to intercept the threat and bring it safely to the ground — delivering high-mobility protection with minimal impact on the surrounding environment.

From a Working Prototype to a Product That Meets Every Team’s Needs

The project began as a working engineering prototype. Before designing anything, I gathered every team's must-haves and requirements, so the product could meet engineering needs and our industrial design goals at the same time: brand language, user experience and form. The aim was to find the point where engineering and design could both deliver.

Design Development

I first worked with the engineering team to understand how the internal components would be stacked and what design constraints each one carried. This helped me identify both the limits on the exterior form and the space available for design.

As Tron Future's first quadcopter, the product offered a great deal of freedom in defining its visual language, so we framed its image and identity around two directions. "Futuristic Armor": sharp-edged, rugged, and durable, inspired by stealth fighters and futuristic armored vehicles. "Aerodynamic Sculpture": fluid, precise lines, in the spirit of FPV drones.

Using hand sketches, digital renderings, and AI-assisted exploration, I quickly explored a wide range of silhouettes. After internal discussions, we decided to converge the design direction around a vocabulary of balance, trustworthiness, durability, and minimalism. Each concept was then tested and refined against both design and engineering constraints.

Regardless of style, what I valued most during the design phase was proportion, stance, and a memorable silhouette. I was aiming for a streamlined, flowing form with a sports car fastback posture. Step by step, I worked with the engineers to adjust the internal stack, achieving this goal without compromising function or performance.

Finally, together with the design director, we finalized the brand language, detail guidelines, and color scheme, delivering a result that satisfied everyone across engineering, marketing, and design.

Design Validation

Perfect synergy of functionality and modularity, featuring interchangeable equipment mounts, a quick-release battery system, and thermal design.

Flight Testing & Validation

From controlled indoor environments to open-field scenarios, each flight test validates the system's autonomous detection, tracking, and net-based capture performance. Iterative testing under real-world conditions ensures reliability across diverse operational environments — refining flight dynamics, sensor accuracy, and structural durability before deployment.

Form Follows Function

The rear-mounted battery module is designed for rapid field replacement — minimizing downtime between missions. The tool-free extraction mechanism allows operators to swap power units in seconds, keeping the system mission-ready with minimal interruption.

Multi-Sensor Gimbal System

Equipped with a high-performance stabilized gimbal integrating visible light, laser rangefinder, and thermal imaging — enabling all-weather target identification, distance measurement, and tracking across day and night operations.

Angled Net Launcher — CG-Aligned Design

The capture net launcher is mounted at a calculated angle rather than horizontal. This ensures the reaction force during net deployment passes through the drone's center of gravity, preventing destabilizing torque that could cause mid-flight rollover. The angled geometry is seamlessly integrated into the body's surface language, turning an engineering constraint into a defining design detail.
