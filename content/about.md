Tzu-I Chang (Joel Chang) is a Taiwanese industrial designer based in St. Louis, Missouri. Specializing in bridging design vision with engineering feasibility to drive complex hardware from concept to mass production.

# Currently Principal Industrial Designer at Tron Future Tech (remote), he has successfully launched 8+ products across networking, defense products, and drone systems. His career began at Gemtek, where he rebuilt the in-house product design language and custom design for global telecom partners. He transitioned into the defense technology sector, refining brand identity and product design to strike a perfect balance between aesthetic distinction, engineering challenges, and reliable manufacturing. Rooted in close collaboration with international clients, R&D, and Asian suppliers, he aligns user needs, product vision, and production constraints early on. He translates complex requirements into clear, memorable, Use-Friendly, and highly manufacturable solutions, bringing a strong foundation in DFM, UX, CMF, and brand identity, backed by extensive hands-on manufacturing experience.

## What I Believe

## For me, industrial design isn’t about aesthetics alone. It’s the process of translating complexity into intuition, and it always starts with the people who use it. I’m not a pure artist, yet my work is shaped by constant aesthetic restraint. I don’t claim to be an engineer, yet my designs are rooted in rigorous engineering logic and manufacturing reality. I act as the bridge between the two, with the user always at the center. “Good design solves problems and ships. Great design brings warmth to cold hardware: turning a product into something people connect with, keep, and find timeless.” ​ ​ Let’s talk design, or anything in between.
