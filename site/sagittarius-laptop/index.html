Laptop

SAGITTARIUS

Stay Professional. Stay Sharp.

Stay Yourself.

Business laptop is designed to be "safe." In trying not to offend anyone, it ends up looking like everyone else. Every meeting is a row of identical silver lids: dependable, yet completely forgettable.

​

This is a self-initiated project, with a deliberately narrow brief.

SAGITTARIUS treats understatement as a refined form of professional presence, rather than a fear of standing out. This is more than a laptop. Its distinctive blue-gray body, finished with platinum trim, embodies an attitude: stay true to yourself, carry your professionalism and personality with you, and let it help you stand out in both work and life.

Sharp Without Shouting

Most business laptops play safe and end up identical. Sagittarius takes the opposite aim. The form borrows a drawn bow's line — arched, taut, drawn inward — so restraint reads as presence, not absence. You know which one is yours from across the office.

Crafted in Restraint

Presented with a minimalist design language, the laptop is built with CNC machining and anodized finishing, complemented by delicate texturing for a distinctive, premium feel. Every detail is carefully crafted, from the A cover to the D cover and even the hinge, reflecting an uncompromising pursuit of refinement.

Restraint Over Detail

To keep the C surface clean and minimal, the vents and speaker grille are concealed along the flat plane at the hinge, between the display and the keyboard.

It speaks through proportion, material, design language, and small details.

Every element has been refined again and again, so the product keeps a distinct personality within its simplicity. It does not need more decoration; instead, it builds a consistent and striking identity through control of proportion, material, and detail.

The Concave Surface as Visual Language

The bow draws its power from the curve that pulls inward. Sagittarius does not push volume outward; it lets the body draw in slightly, holding the tension within the surface. Quiet, yet ready to release.

​

The keyboard and edges follow the same design language as the sides, so the elements stay consistent rather than competing with one another.

CMF Strategy

Low chroma color, material, and the play of light are what do the talking.

The body is built on an anodized aluminum magnesium alloy, with a matte texture and a fine #200 bead blasted finish, outlined by a brushed metal trim that draws out its premium feel. Curved edge transitions guide the flow of light and shadow, and every chamfer is given a secondary anodizing pass to sharpen its refinement and elevate the overall character.

Modern Urban Elite

Tranquil Blue-Gray & Platinum

Designed for the urban elite who pursue lifestyle quality and unique individuality. The multi-layered blue-gray tones break away from the stereotypes of traditional business equipment, complemented by platinum-colored details that evoke a sense of calm, confident modern charm. It seamlessly blends work and leisure, highlighting the user's unconventional and discerning taste.

Classic Executive

Obsidian Black & Matte Gold

Crafted specifically for the composed and sophisticated executive. The deep obsidian black chassis exudes a subtle yet premium metallic texture, while the refined gold accents on the hinge perfectly embody an aura of prestige and grandeur. With every fold and opening, it radiates impeccable professionalism and decisive leadership.
